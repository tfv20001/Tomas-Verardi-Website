## Automated Resistance Spot Welder – Pursuit Aerospace
*Senior Design Project & Manufacturing Internship | Aug 2024 – Aug 2025*

### Overview
Led design of a fully automated resistance spot welding system to replace a slow, defect-prone manual process for jet engine flowline components.

### My Role
- Took ownership of **CAD, prototyping, and testing**.  
- Redesigned multiple subassemblies for **durability, usability, and safety**.  
- Developed and optimized a **novel dual-wheel weld head**:  
  - Spring-based force control (Hooke’s law system).  
  - Structure changed from steel → Delrin (nonconductive, machinable, strong enough for loads).  
  - Utilized Class 3 copper and copper lubricants to reduce wear and galling.  

### Results
- **Cycle time reduced**: 25 min → 4 min (80% faster).  
- **Defects eliminated**: ~50 reworks per part → 0.  
- **Durability**: 100,000+ welds without major issues.  
- **Operator usability**: hands-free, intuitive to run.  
- Won **1st Place – UConn Senior Design Competition (2025)**.  
- Adopted directly into Pursuit Aerospace’s production flowline.
