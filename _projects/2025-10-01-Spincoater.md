## Substrate Spincoater – UConn
*UConn Honors Research | Sep 2025 – Ongoing*

### Overview
Designing a custom spincoater to accomidate the automated manufacturing of DEA's (soft robotics)

### My Role
- **Designed and prototyped a custom spin-coater** for thin-film coating applications, focused on simplicity and reliability.  
- **Integrated motor control with adjustable RPM** to enable consistent and repeatable coating results.  
- **Created modular sample holders** to accommodate different substrate sizes while keeping costs low.
